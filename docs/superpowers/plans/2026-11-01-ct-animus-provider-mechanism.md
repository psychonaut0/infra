## Acceptance for this review checkpoint

- Generated candidate matches its exact template/expression inputs; expressions
  compile and tagged YAML parses without ambiguous managed mapping lookups.
- Separate named flows leave the global administrator path unchanged. No user or
  membership is seeded; password/TOTP are unconditional. Guards use mandatory
  positive Prompt validation, uncached ALL/failure-false, not conditional Deny.
  Submitted fields never supply time or proof.
- Entry cannot renew or inherit a timing envelope. Final checks preserve its
  conservative bound, reject unsupported process modes, and do not mark recovery.
- Experimental policy ID cannot accidentally activate production proof.
- Initial application issuance always denies, failure-false. Optional issuance
  requires explicit separate output and exact recipe review.
- Positive app access checks current qualified login. Authorization Prompts bind
  exact subject/event/parameters before and after consent; later atomicity is
  unproven and tested through redirect identity switches.
- Source replay distinguishes inline mutation from fork copy isolation and
  records the runner-failure Deny-skip limitation explicitly.
- The pending-completion control reproduces why authorize/token-only hold or
  an empty inventory without a complete owner drain is insufficient.
- Private fixture/actor have internal networks, no ports/socket/shared live
  volumes, separate operator/client custody, file/stdin secrets, parked redirects
  and bounded safe output. Exact hashes require review before execution.

## Required gates after this checkpoint

1. Review exact isolated fixture, private secret transport and supported HTTP/API
   actor recipe. No host ports, external networks, shared volumes or Docker socket.
2. Observe actual pinned worker process/context/session behavior and immutable
   timing through resumed/new/restored/concurrent executions and factor pauses.
3. Explore the original-grant invariant in the separately reviewed issuance
   experiment. No recheck at code/refresh conversion is not inherently fatal if
   the original chain was qualified. Missing marker alone is insufficient for
   ordinary API safety. Test false/error/timeout, stale/operator SSO, every redirect
   identity switch and event replacement; refresh retains old bound or loses proof.
4. Inventory real browser requests and independently review the exact public
   allowlist/default denial plus full issuance hold/drain/revocation procedure.
5. Qualify controlled password+one-use-code recovery, replacement validation,
   final-factor self-service denial, complete invalidation and same-principal
   fresh login. No forgotten-password email claim without qualified delivery.
6. After revocation/reopen, resume anonymous plans after password/TOTP and after
   final Prompt before User Login, plus authz plans after final Prompt before
   fulfillment. Empty session inventory misses partial plans. No qualified epoch/
   invalidation exists yet; a failing experiment keeps readiness closed.
